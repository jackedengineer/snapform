# Workspace plans and Razorpay test-mode billing

Replaces the creator trial with per-workspace Free and Pro plans ([ADR-023](../decisions/ADR-023-workspace-plans.md)). Pure rules are in `packages/contracts/src/plans.ts`; migration 0041 enforces the same limits in the database. Nothing here is applied, deployed or charged.

**Codes.** Publishing a fourth live form on Free returns 409 `LIVE_FORM_LIMIT`. A start or save that would exceed the monthly allowance plus unexpired reservations returns 409 `FORM_NOT_ACCEPTING`; the collector maps it to its closed state ("This form isn't accepting responses right now"). Billing errors are `BILLING_NOT_CONFIGURED` 503, `BILLING_ALREADY_ACTIVE` and `BILLING_NOT_ACTIVE` 409, `BILLING_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` 502.

**Razorpay.** Subscriptions are created with Basic auth from the test key and cancelled at cycle end ([create](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/payments/subscriptions/create-subscription/), [cancel](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/payments/subscriptions/cancel-subscription/)). Checkout loads `checkout.razorpay.com/v1/checkout.js` only on the billing page ([integration guide](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/integration-guide/)); success only starts polling for the webhook-confirmed plan. The webhook verifies the hex HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body, dedupes `x-razorpay-event-id` and tolerates reordering ([validation](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test/), [subscription events](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/subscriptions/)). `RAZORPAY_MODE` (`test` or `live`) selects the key prefix and that mode's plan variables; live requires `SNAPFORM_ENV=production`, and any mismatch or placeholder plan leaves billing unconfigured. USD plans are optional; without them checkout and `/pricing` offer INR only. Setup: [billing runbook](../runbooks/billing.md).

**Browser checklist (synthetic local data, after 0041 is applied).**

- Normal: Free workspace meter reads usage; publish three forms, fourth shows the 3 live form message; Upgrade opens Razorpay test Checkout; a test card payment turns the page to Pro after the webhook; Cancel Pro keeps Pro until period end; `/pricing` toggles monthly or annual and INR or USD.
- Power: keyboard through radio groups and confirm step; back and forward; double-click Upgrade reuses one subscription; dismiss Checkout and retry; refresh during polling; 320 to 1440 px widths; usage reset day near month end in a non-UTC time zone.
- Adversarial: editor and viewer get no Upgrade and 403 from the API; cross-origin POST is refused; forged, replayed, reordered and oversized webhooks change nothing; a respondent at the limit sees the closed state; CSP blocks Razorpay hosts on every other route; no Razorpay script before the owner clicks Upgrade.

**Limitations.** Free can be multiplied by creating workspaces. A workspace deleted with an active subscription is not cancelled at Razorpay automatically. Pro lapses at period end during payment retries, before `halted`. Team invites, branding, uploads, embed and the public badge read the exposed flags later; the collector does not yet receive `badgeRequired`.
