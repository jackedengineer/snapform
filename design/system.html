Preflight before sharing

Inspect the exact effective payload, test the destination, and invalidate the test receipt when its mapping changes.

External side effects are not universally exactly-once. Unknown outcomes need reconciliation.

A complete V1, with a bounded promise.

Planning estimate: 960–1,200 founder engineering hours / 32–40 weeks at 30 focused hours per week, including contingency. No product functionality has been built.

Required for the market release

  • Build and publish safelyTemplates, stable fields, conditional paths, save/conflict states, immutable versions and branding.
  • Collect a trusted recordMobile fields, optional bounded conversation, candidate evidence, direct review, private files and durable submit.
  • Finish the business jobResults/export, email and signed webhook, visible recovery, payload preflight and supported migration.
  • Operate a paid productSmall-team roles, billing/limits/cancellation, privacy/deletion, abuse protection, monitoring and tested recovery.

Expansion after evidence

Payments inside forms, bookings, signing, voice, offline, advanced workflows and enterprise controls need their own commercial case and release gates.

V1.1: activation and connectors.
V1.5: branding and collection breadth.
V2: one adjacent workflow.
V3: enterprise/vertical commitments when funded.
